# Athichaar

## 1. Naanami

Naanami dhansahnamia,
Charahnami thavami thahaya viriyami,
Aayarahna aayaaro,
Iya eso panchahaa bhahnio.

Gnaanaachaar, dharshanaachaar, chaarithraachaar, thapaachaar, viriyaachaar
E pancha vidhh aachaar maahi anero ...

*Je koi athichaar paksha(samvatsari) dhivas maahi*
*Sukshma baadhar, jaanthaa ajaanthaa huvo hoi,*
*The savihu, man vachan kaayaa-e kari, michhaami dukdam.*

## 2. Gnaanaachaar

Thathra gnaanaa chaare aatthh athichaar.
Kaale vina-e bahumaane,
Uva haane thahaya ninha vane,
Vanjana athha thadhu bhaye,
Attha viho naan maayaaro.

Gnaan kaar veraae bhanio ganio nahi, akaare bhanio
Vinai-hin, bahumaan-hin, yog updhhaan-hin.
Aneraa kane bhani, anero guru kahio
Dhev guru vaandhne, parikamne, sajaaya karthaa, bhanthaa ganthaa, kuro akshar kaane maathraa-e adhhiko ochho bhanio.
Suthra kuru kahiu, arth kooro kahio, thadhu bhai kuraa kahiaa.

Bhanine visaariyaa, saadhhu thane dhharme kaajo, ana udhariye daando, ana parilehe vasthi ana shodhhe ana pavese asajaai anojaai-maahe, shree dhasvai kaalika, pramuk sidhhaantha, bhanio ganio.
Shraavak thane dharma, sthhaviraavali, parikamnaa, updesh maalaa, pramukh shidhhaantha, bhanio ganio.
Kaar veraa kaajo, ana udhariye paddhio,
Gnaano pagran, paati pothhi, tthauni kauli, naukaar vaari, saapdaa saapdi, dhasthari vahi,
Oliyaa pramuk prathiye, pag laagyo thhuk laagyu thhuke kari akshar maanjyo oshishe dhhariyo kane chhathaa aahaar nihaar kidhho.
Gnaan dhravya bhaksha thaa upekshaa kidhhi.

Pragnaa paraadhhe vinasiyo, vinsthaa uvekhiyo, chhathi shakthi-e saar sambhaar na kidhhi.
Gnaana vantha prathiye, dhvesh mathsar chinthaviyo, avagnaa aashaathanaa kidhhi.
Koi prathiye bhanthaa ganthaa anthraai kidhho. Aapraa jaan panaa thano, garva chinthavio.
Mathi gnaan, shrutha gnaan, audhhi gnaan, manapari aavagnaan, kevar gnaan - e pancha vidhh gnaan thani asadhahnaa kidhhi.
Koi thothro bobro hasio, vitha kario, anya thhaa praru panaa kidhhi.

Gnaanaachaar vishaio anero, *je koi athichaar …*

## 3. Dharshanaachaar

Dharshanaa chaare aatth athichaar.
Nisankia nikankhia,
Nivithigichhaa amuudhha ditthia,
Uvavuha thhiri karne,
Vachhala pabhaave attha.

Dhev guru dharma thane vise, nisank panu na kidhhu. Thathaa ekaanth nischai na kidhho.
Dhharma sabandhhi ar thane vise nisandheha budhhi dhhari nahi.
Saddhhi saadhhvinaa mal marine gaathra dhekhi, dhurganchhaa nipjaavi.
Koo chaarithriyaa dhekhi, chaarithriyaa upar abhaav huvo, mithhyaa thvee thani pujaa prabhaavnaa dhekhi,moddh dhrasti panu kidhhu.

Thathhaa sangha maahe, gurna vantha thani anupabruhnaa kidhhi.
a-sthirikaran, a-vaathsalya, a-prithi, a-bhakthi nipjaavi, a-bahumaan kidhhu.
Dhev dhravya bhakshitha upekshith,
Pragnaa paraadhhe vinasiyo, vinsthaa uvekhiyo, chhathi shakthi-e saar sambhaar na kidhhi.

Thathhaa saadhharmik saathhe, kalaha karmabandhha kidhho.
a-dhhothi, a-shtapar mukh kosh paakhe dhev pooja kidhhi.
Bimba prathiye vaas koopi dhhoopa dhhaanu kalash thano tthabako laagyo, bimba haathh thhaki paaryu.

Usaas nisaas laagyo, dhehare upaasraye malaslesh maadhik lohiyu. Dheharaa maahe, haasiya khel, keli kuthuhal, aahaar nihaar kidhhaa.
Paan sopaari nivedhia khaadhhaa. tthaunaayariya haathh tthaki paaryaa.
Parilehavaa visaariya, jin bhavane choraasi aashaathanaa, guru guruni prathiye thethris aashaathanaa kidhhi hoi. Guru vachan thahathi kari pari vajyu nahi.

Dharshanaachaar vishaio anero, *je koi athichaar …*

## 4. Chaarithraachaar

Chaarithraare aatth athichaar.
Panihaan joga jootho,
Pancha-him sami-ihim, thi-him, duthi-him,
Esa chaarithaayaaro,
Attha viha hoi naayavo.

Iriyaa samithi - the ana joye hindia.
Bhaashaa samithi - the saavadhh vachan bolyaa.
Eshnaa samithi - the thrun dagal ana paani asu jathu lidhhu.
Aadhaan bhhanda matha nikhe vanaa samithi - the aashan shayan, upakaran, maathru pramukh, ana poonji jivaakul bhoomi kaaye, mukyu lidhhu.
Paarishtthaa panikaa samithi - the mar muthra slesh maadhik, ana poonji jivaakul bhoomi kaaye, paratthavyu.

Mano gupthi - man maa aartha rodhra dhhyaan dhhyaayaa.
Vachan gupthi - saavadh vachan boliyaa.
Kaay gupthi - sarir ana pari lehyu halaavyu ana poonje betthaa.
E asttha pravachan maathaa the, saadhoo thane dharme sadhayva, ane shraavak thane dhharme saamaayk posaha lidhhe.
Rudi pere paaryaa nahi, khandahna viraadhhanaa hoo-i.

Chaarithraachaar vishaio anero, *je koi athichaar …*

## 5. Vishe Shathaha

Vishe shathaha shraavak thane dhharme, shree samyakthva moola baar vrath, samyaktha thanaa paanch athichaar.
Sankaa kankhavigichhaa, shankaa shree arihantha thanaa bara,
Athisai gnaan lakshmi, gaambhiriyaadhik goon, shaashvathi prathimaa, chaarithriyaa naa chaarithra,
Shree jin vachan thano sandheha kidhho.

Aakaankshaa, brahmaa, vishnu, maheshvar, kshethrapaal, go-go, aaspaal, paadhar dhevthaa, gothra dhevthaa, graha poojaa, vinaayak, hanumanth, sugriv, vaali, naaha.
Ithyeva maadhik, dhesh naga gaam gothra nagri joojoo aa.

Dhev dheharaanaa prabhaav dhekhi, rog aathanka kashta aave. Iha lock par lock aarthhe, poojiyaa maanyaa, sidhha vinaayak, jiraa oolaane, maanyoo ichhyoo.

Bovdhha saankhiyaadhik, sanyaasi, bhharadaa, bhhagatha, lingiyaa, jogiyaa jogi, dharvesha, aneraa dharshaniyaa thano kashta manthra chamathakaar dhekhi.
Paramaarthha jaaniyaa vinaa bhoolaaviyaa mohiyaa kooshaasthra shikyaa saambharyaa.

Shraadh samvathsari hori barev maahi poonam
Ajaa parvo
Preth bij
Gauri thrij
Vinaayaak chothha
Naag panchami
Jilanaa chhatti
Seel saathmi
Dhhruv aatthmi
Nauli naumi
Ahavaa dhashmi
Vrath agiyaarasi
Vathsa baarsi
Dhhan therasi
Ananth chaudhasi

Amaa vaasiyaa aadhithyaa vaar utharaayan naivedh kidhhaa.
Navo dhak yaag bhog uthaaranaa, kidhhaa karaaviyaa anumodhiyaa.
Pipane paani, ghaaliya ghalaaviyaa:
Ghara, baahir, kshethre, khale, koove, tharaave, nadhiye, dhrahe, vaavi-e, samudhre, koonde, pooniya hethu snaan, kidhaa karaaviyaa anumodhiyaa.

Grahan sanisvar, maaha maase, nauraathri-e naahiyaa.
Ajaahanaa thhaapiyaa, aneraa vrath vratholaa kidhhaa karaaviyaa.
Vithi gichhaa, dharma sabandhhiyaa far thane vise, sandheha kidhho.

Jin arihantha dhharmanaa aagaar, visvo pakaar saahar, moksha maanganaa dhaathaar, ishyaa goon bhani, na maaniyaa na poojiyaa.
Mahaasathi mahaathamaani iha lock parlock sabandhhiyaa, bhog vaanchhitha poojaa kidhhi.

Rog aathank kashta aave, khina vachan bhog maaniyaa. Mahaathamaanaa bhaath paani mal shobhaa thani nindhaa kidhhi.
Koo chaarithriyaa dhekhi, chaarithriyaa upar koobhaav hoovo.
Mithhyaa thvee thani poojaa prabhaavnaa dheki prashanshaa kidhhi.
Prithi maandi dhaakshinya lage thehano dharma maanyo kidhho.

Shree samyaktha tha vishaio anero, *je koi athichaar …*

## 6. Pahele sthhul

Pahele sthhul praanvathi paath, viraman vrathe paanch athichaar.
Vahabandhhi chhavichhe-e, dhvee padh chathuspadh prathiye, rees vase gaaddho ghaav ghaaliyo, gaaddhe bandhhan baandhhiyu.

Adhhik bhaar ghaaliyo, nirlanchhan karma  kidhhaa. Chaaraa paani thani veraa-e, saar sambhhaar na kidhhi. Lehane dhehane kinahi prathiye langhaaviyo. Thene bhookhe aapre jamiy(ae).
Kanhe rahi maraaviyo, bandhhi khaani ghalaaviyo, sariyaa dhhaan thaaure naakhiya dharaaviyaa bhharadaaviyaa shodhhi navaavariyaa indhhan chhaana ana shodhhiyaa baariyaa the maahi saap vichhi khajooraa sarauraa maakar joovaa gingodaa saahathaa moohaa dhoohaviyaa, roore sthhaanak-e na mookiyaa.
